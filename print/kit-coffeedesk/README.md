# Kit for Kawiarnia Coffeedesk

Six cafés in Warsaw: Wilcza 42, Próżna 7, Tamka 2, Pańska 97, Nowy Świat 6/12 and Answear Concept Store
(Fabryka Norblina). Instagram @kawiarniacoffeedesk (22.7K followers). Coffeedesk belongs to All Good S.A.,
which also runs the coffeedesk.pl shop and a B2B business that supplies cafés around Poland.
No café app found; the online shop has its own points programme.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `card-coffeedesk-indigo-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Indigo: faint curls of steam behind the stamps, every stamp is their cup |
| `card-coffeedesk-pink-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Pink: indigo stamps with their cup |
| `propozycja-coffeedesk.pdf` | 3 A4 pages in Polish: the two cards, one card for six cafés, the panel with results per café, Pro (79 €) or a Plus pilot in 3 cafés (source `sales/coffeedesk.html`) |

The cup is redrawn from their Instagram picture (`print/coffeedesk-look.js`), not their original file.
Rebuild: `node print/make-example-cards.js coffeedesk`, then `node print/make-pdfs.js coffeedesk`.
