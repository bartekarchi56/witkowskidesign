# Kit for STREFA Cafe&Food&Cocktails

Café and restaurant in Bydgoszcz: specialty coffee, breakfasts, lunch (12:00 to 17:00), cocktails,
company Christmas dinners. Instagram @strefa_cafe (4.6K followers). Older listings put a Strefa Cafe at
ul. Długa 11 with a vegan menu and yoga; the current menu is different, so check the address before a visit.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `card-strefa-peach-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Peach, like their logo: green stamps with their lotus cup |
| `card-strefa-green-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Green: peach stamps |

The cup and the wordmark are redrawn from their Instagram picture (`print/strefa-look.js`).
Rebuild: `node print/make-example-cards.js strefa`.
