# Kit for Landschaft

Specialty café at ul. Gimnazjalna 6, Bydgoszcz: "Cøffee is life!", coffee and matcha, an autumn menu,
talks and exhibitions. Instagram @landschaft_bydgoszcz (7.6K followers). Their logo is the name in
thin, round, bronze letters on the sign over the door; their picture is a spindle-back chair.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `card-landschaft-white-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Light like the facade: their name as on the sign, in bronze; bronze stamps with the chair |
| `card-landschaft-dark-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Graphite: the name and the stamps in light bronze |

The name is redrawn as lines from a photo of the sign, the chair from their Instagram picture (`print/landschaft-look.js`).
Rebuild: `node print/make-example-cards.js landschaft`.
