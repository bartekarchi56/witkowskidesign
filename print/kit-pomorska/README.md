# Kit for Pomorska 15

Specialty café at Pomorska 15, Bydgoszcz: open every day 10:00 to 20:00, hot bagels, events
(Bydgoskie dzierganie), barista courses, dog friendly. Instagram @pomorska15 (6.9K followers).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `card-pomorska-white-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | White, like their logo: black stamps with their takeaway cup |
| `card-pomorska-black-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Black: white stamps with the cup |

The cup and "POMORSKA 15 · CAFE" are redrawn from their Instagram picture (`print/pomorska-look.js`).
Rebuild: `node print/make-example-cards.js pomorska`.
