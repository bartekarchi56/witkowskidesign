# Kit for Kofeina

Example cards for Kofeina in Bydgoszcz (instagram.com/kofeina_coffee), a friend's café,
in the same city as Cukiernia Sowa's head office. Black and white like their brand.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `card-kofeina-simple-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Simple: their KOFEINA logo, plain black dots (no texture), 10 stamps, free coffee |
| `card-kofeina-dark-iphone.png` / `-android.png` | Black: their storefront drawing faint behind white dots, 8 stamps |
| `brochure-kofeina-pl.pdf` | The Polish brochure with their name |

`art/` holds their logo and storefront drawing, made transparent from their images, used only for this proposal.
Rebuild: `node print/make-example-cards.js` (designs in `print/example-cards.html`).
